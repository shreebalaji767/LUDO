HOW TO PLAY

VOID LUDO supports 2–10 local players. Any player can be controlled by a human or the computer.

1
Roll the dice. A token normally enters the track when you roll a 6.
2
Choose a highlighted token after a roll.
3
Move the token clockwise around the board.
4
Landing on an opponent can send that token back to its yard.
5
Safe cells cannot be used for captures.
6
Reach the center home path to complete your token.
7
Complete all four tokens to win.
